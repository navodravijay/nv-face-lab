PRIVACY FIRST
YOUR CAMERA. YOUR CONTROL.

YOUR FACE ISN'T
OUR DATA.

We do not implement account creation, image recording, biometric identification, frame uploads, or a database. Your camera frames are processed by MediaPipe in your browser for this visualization; manual effect previews work without camera access. Camera permission is requested only after your action; Stop releases the video tracks, and leaving the tab stops the camera.

External connections: This version downloads MediaPipe code from jsDelivr and AI models from Google storage. Google's MediaPipe documentation notes that usage and performance metrics may be sent to Google. No software can guarantee absolute privacy or security; review the dependencies and host configuration before production use.
